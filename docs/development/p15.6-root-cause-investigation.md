# P15.6 native replay investigation

Accuracy recovery remains unresolved. This change makes one measured,
pixel-preserving preprocessing performance correction. It does not claim that
the scanner now correctly recognizes these sheets.

## B. Controlled experiments A–F

### A. Four-point, multi-point and production selection

The existing ArucoCaptureReplayHarness runs four-point-only, 16-corner RANSAC,
and actual production selection on identical encoded JPEGs. Its full selected
alignment results are now exposed so downstream replay reuses those warps;
it does not decode/align again to obtain measurements. Each experimental path
retains its own detection, as in the existing harness. Production still detects
once and warps once. Source corners, matrices, residuals, masks, selection and
timings, where estimation ran, are retained in the
[six baseline reports](evidence/p15_6/).

Baseline alignment reaches recognition for four of six captures. Of the four
multi-point candidates, three fail rejected-corner validation; tilted mixed
strokes has localized-uncertainty acceptance. None qualifies as a reliable
multi-point preference, so the production transform remains four-point.
No rejected estimator is promoted to normal recognition.

For the blank sheet, four-point mean/max corner residuals are 4.691/15.228 px;
multi-point residuals are 40.581/173.917 px, with per-marker support `[4,0,2,4]`.
Four-point's four fitted residuals are near zero; those values do not establish
correct bubble positioning. Multi-point's zero support at one marker must not
be concealed by a more favorable local result.

The existing printed-outline evaluator was also run with a wider, explicitly
diagnostic 24-pixel search. Its default four-pixel search saturates on this
capture and understates displacement. Its observations were back-projected to
source coordinates and projected through **both stored matrices**, using the
existing projection/evaluation utilities. This needs no additional warp.

| Blank-sheet outline landmark displacement, canonical pixels | Four-point | Rejected multi-point, diagnostic projection only |
|---|---:|---:|
| Mean | 9.452 | 8.830 |
| P95 | 17.720 | 15.197 |
| Maximum | 19.925 | 15.440 |
| Top-region mean | 6.809 | 8.611 |
| Middle-region mean | 13.316 | 9.151 |

These are algorithmically observed outlines, visually inspected in overlays,
not manually verified ground truth. Search bias/radius fitting remains a limit.
All answer bubbles are in the top/middle page regions; there is no bottom answer
region in this sheet. [Raw observations](evidence/p15_6/wide-outline-evidence.json)
and [both-transform comparison](evidence/p15_6/independent_projection_comparison.json)
are retained. The small overall improvement does not make this rejected
multi-point transform safe, and neither transform demonstrates adequate local
registration for the narrow sampling annulus. No production policy changed.

Two controls isolate input changes without changing production configuration:

|---|---:|---:|
| Current 1400-pixel detection/subpixel refinement | 4/6 | 13/44 |

These counts include ambiguous/multiple/blank wrong outcomes as incorrect.
Rejected captures are reported separately, not counted as evaluated questions.
Different completion subsets make the conditional ratios unsuitable as a
ranked accuracy benchmark. The controls do not justify enabling full resolution
or reverting corner refinement. The second control changes refinement only,
not every P14.4 detector default.

### B. Actual sampling positions

Published printed radius is 25 px, center exclusion 12.5 px, and interior radius
17 px. The 4.5-pixel annulus covers 21.24% of the ideal full disk before pixel
discretization. For the inspected bubbles it contains 428 actual sample pixels.
The local paper annulus extends from 31.25 to 50 px, with neighbor/foreground
exclusion retained.

Blank-sheet printed-outline displacement reaches approximately 20 pixels.
Examples include Q3 option 4: expected center `(555,635)`, observed outline
center approximately `(556,645)`, observed radius 22.5 px. Q11 option A is
approximately `(351,1416)` versus expected `(345,1435)`. These displacements
exceed the outline-to-inner-ROI clearance. The measurement masks consequently
overlap printed outlines/labels, despite complete sample coverage and a
numerically valid homography.

- [Blank sheet ROI zoom](evidence/p15_6/unmarked_roi_zoom.png)
- [Mixed-stroke ROI zoom](evidence/p15_6/mixstroke_roi_zoom.png)

Red: expected printed radius; green: measurement outer boundary; magenta:
excluded center; cyan: paper-baseline outer boundary. The full local output
also contains `*_sampled_pixels.png`, coloring the exact sampled binary pixels,
not just expected center points. Whole canonical/normalized/binary images are
in `build/p156-before`, with corresponding unchanged outputs in
`build/p156-after-complete`.

This establishes wrong local pixels for several false selections. It does not
establish whether their origin is paper curvature, printing distortion, lens
distortion, or remaining marker-localization error. No per-bubble recentering,
geometry rewrite, or uncalibrated new gate was introduced.

### D. Historical recognition requirements

Six additional controlled tests extend the existing labeled-bubble fixtures:
half fill, crescent, distributed dots, X and light fill remain selected under
equivalent measurement-ready inputs. Existing full-fill/blank/multiple tests
remain. These tests do not include native preprocessing or establish physical
accuracy.

A separate test demonstrates an information limit: a dot wholly inside the
label-excluded center changes the image but produces zero measured foreground
and remains BLANK. Thus arbitrary centered-dot recovery cannot be claimed for
this labeled format. Exclusion predates P15; changing it without blank-label
controls could create false selections. No ROI dimensions changed.

### E. Preprocessing performance

The pinned `Mat.fromList` calls `data.cast<int>()` before constructing VecU8.
That generic list view prevents the typed buffer's bulk-copy path. Both the old
and corrected paths ultimately call the same copying `Mat_NewFromBytes` native
constructor. On the actual 6,237,000-byte canonical buffer, five paired profile
measurements showed approximately 101 ms via Mat.fromList versus 6.3 ms through
VecU8.fromList → Mat.fromVec. All five resulting native-matrix hashes matched.

Correction: retain the typed bytes for the vector bulk copy. The vector is
disposed after Mat creation and also on failure. Native Mat ownership and PNG
input behavior remain unchanged; no new persistent buffer/detector is introduced.
The responsible call dates to P11.2, so this is longstanding overhead rather
than evidence that P15 introduced the reported 1151.8 ms latency.

| Same-image profile replay | Before preprocessing ms | After ms |
|---|---:|---:|
| Mixed strokes | 536.657 | 453.292 |
| Tilted full marks | 534.208 | 448.569 |
| Tilted mixed strokes | 552.808 | 456.385 |
| Unmarked | 540.885 | 454.905 |
| Descriptive median | 538.771 | 454.0985 |

Observed reduction: 83–96 ms per completed scan; median reduction 84.6725 ms
(15.7%). This is one paired run per image on one device, not a statistical
cross-device benchmark. Native denoise (~107–109 ms), normalization (~122–124 ms),
thresholding (~153–160 ms) and statistics (~19–20 ms) are still substantial.
They were not changed speculatively.

All twelve canonical/normalized/binary PNG pairs are byte-identical; all four
measurement arrays and selected-answer/state arrays match exactly. The
[comparison report](evidence/p15_6/comparison.json) contains image identity,
before/after times, hashes and equality results. Recognition did not improve
or degrade in this replay. The historical 565.2 ms capture delay and 1151.8 ms
preprocessing sample are separate observations, not the before timings above.
Live throughput and capture-to-result latency were not measured in this offline
replay. Snapshot `total` excludes the previously performed alignment; replay
reports label downstream-only duration explicitly.

### F. Component cooperation

- The exact published geometry and packet/template identity were recovered;
  no newer layout was interpreted as an older one and no old capture was reused.
- Source image dimensions, marker-local corner order and source/canonical
  coordinate remapping remain in the existing backend. Photographs include a
  rotated sheet; required-ID failure is reported rather than silently rotated
  or assigned a replacement ID.
- Normal production retains one decode/session, one selected warp, one
  preprocessing pass and one recognition pass. Experimental alternate decodes
  belong to the existing replay harness, not the camera path.
- Native preprocessing outputs retain contiguous grayscale and 0/1 binary
  contracts. On-device offset-view/non-square tests pass against the Dart
  reference: maximum normalized difference 1, binary differences 0; disabling
  filtering/normalization preserves the original bytes exactly.
- `SCANCOMPLETED` describes pipeline completion. ScanAcceptancePolicy can then
  reject blank, multiple or ambiguous results. That is not evidence of stale
  state or a swallowed alignment failure. All six baseline attempts fail final
  application acceptance, including those that reached recognition.
- Camera/document scheduling was not modified. Host camera/overlay tests pass,
  but no new physical live-preview FPS or capture-readiness run was performed.
- The normal `lib/main.dart` app was rebuilt, installed and launched after the
  experiment. Saved artifacts/packet metadata remain intact.

## D. Changed files and risks

- `opencv_preprocessing_backend.dart`: the only production algorithm-path
  change; faster byte transfer, same native constructor and pixels.
- `aruco_capture_replay_harness.dart`: expose full experimental selections for
  warp reuse; no production selection/acceptance change.
- `lib/debug/p15_6_experiment.dart`, dedicated entry point and tool wrapper:
  task-specific native replay, existing snapshots, actual pixel masks,
  existing stage timings and the missing input-copy microbenchmark. Production
  does not call this entry point.
- `tool/p15_6_outline.dart`, `tool/summarize_p15_6.py`: reproduce independent
  landmark projections and aggregate saved reports; no recognition algorithm.
- Three existing test files: buffer ownership/parity, replay warp reuse and
  controlled marking-style coverage.
- `test/fixtures/p15_6` and `docs/development/evidence/p15_6`: exact artifact,
  supplied keys and reviewable evidence. Original JPEGs stay in the supplied
  folder and are not silently committed.

The typed-vector correction depends on the pinned opencv_dart copying
constructor; a package migration must rerun parity/ownership tests. Accuracy
risks predate this correction and remain visible; no artificial success or
blank-page acceptance has been added.

## E. Before/after and F. unresolved work

Alignment completion stays 4/6; exact recognition stays 13/44 among completed
images; final application acceptance stays 0/6. Blank-sheet output remains
incorrect: only Q4 is blank, with seven questions returning selected marks and
three remaining ambiguous. The performance correction preserves these results;
it must not be described as scanner accuracy recovery.

Remaining evidenced work:

1. Establish why printed bubble centers differ from the selected projective
   alignment. Obtain a paired, rigidly flattened sheet capture, the exact packet
   print job/scaling record, verify raster geometry against the recovered base
   PDF, and obtain independent source-pixel bubble-center
   annotations. Distinguish printing/non-planarity/lens effects from localization
   before selecting a geometry correction.
2. Resolve missing/duplicate marker observations on the two rejected photos
   without ignoring IDs or bypassing safety checks.
3. Inspect faint and central strokes with independently verified alignment;
   contrast loss versus exclusion cannot be resolved by threshold tuning alone.
4. Export the **actual new failed-scan bundle** for the original 22:58:33 Q3
   ambiguity if that exact failure is to be reproduced. The six supplied later
   photographs are useful evidence, but not that original still.
5. Perform a live Android camera/preview/readiness run and repeat profile
   measurements; no live FPS or capture-latency improvement is claimed.
