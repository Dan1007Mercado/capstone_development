# P13.9.1 Multiple-Choice Classification Hardening

## Physical symptom

On the printed P11.1 validation fixture, page 2 aligned and resolved to the
correct PageId, while checklist questions Q6 and Q7 could become `AMBIGUOUS`.
Darkening some marks could then produce `BLANK`. Pages 1 and 3 scanned
correctly.

## Geometry finding

No geometry change was required. Q5, Q6, and Q7 use the same authoritative
page 2 `BubbleRegion` collection produced by `LayoutEngine`. The classifier
matches measurements to those regions by page, question, and option identity;
there is no Q6/Q7-specific positional path. A regression test now locks this
contract.

## Root cause

`BubbleEvidenceEvaluator` already classifies a `possibleMark` only when the
bubble reaches `minimumCombinedScore` and at least two independent minimum
evidence signals. This is the P13.8 corroborated-mark rule.

The old multiple-choice policy nevertheless accepted only `strongMark` and
`clearBlank`. A credible `possibleMark` therefore made the entire checklist
`AMBIGUOUS`, even though exclusive-choice handling could promote such evidence
when it was sufficiently separated.

## Independent-bubble checklist semantics

Each multiple-choice bubble is now interpreted independently:

- `invalid` makes the question `INVALID`.
- `conflicting` or `unreliable` makes the question `AMBIGUOUS`.
- `clearBlank` remains unselected.
- `strongMark` is selected.
- `possibleMark` is selected because it already passed the corroborated P13.8
  credibility rule.

After those decisions, no selections produce `BLANK`; one or more selections
produce `MARKED`. A multiple-choice question never produces `MULTIPLE`, and
selected option IDs retain template/PageLayout order.

The bubble diagnostics remain intact, including fill ratio, normalized
darkness, darkness contrast, combined score, evidence category, quality, and
the checklist-specific selected state. An ambiguous result therefore still
identifies the exact conflicting or unreliable bubble in scanner review.

## Thresholds

No classifier thresholds, weights, ROI measurements, preprocessing behavior,
or quality limits changed. This phase changes only the multiple-choice policy
for an existing credible `possibleMark` category.

## Regression coverage

Deterministic tests cover blank, one/many strong marks, one/many possible
marks, strong plus possible, conflict, unreliable and invalid quality, print
outline noise, unsupported faint evidence, ordering, and the prohibition on
`MULTIPLE` for checklists.

The P11.1 page 2 regression uses synthetic evidence representing dark pen,
partial fill, an X mark, moderate pencil, and untouched printed outlines for
Q6/Q7. Synthetic evidence does not replace printed-sheet testing.

## Physical retest

On the Infinix X6711:

1. Use the existing published `p11-1-scanner-validation` version 1 artifact
   and its existing printed page 2.
2. On Q6, mark two options using black or blue pen: one filled mark and one X.
3. On Q7, mark two options: one partial fill and one clearly visible moderate
   pencil mark.
4. Leave every other Q6/Q7 bubble untouched.
5. Scan page 2 through Real Scanner Validation and inspect the expanded Q6/Q7
   bubble diagnostics.
6. Confirm Q6 and Q7 are `MARKED`, both intended option IDs are retained in
   template order, and outline-only bubbles remain `clearBlank`.
7. Repeat under even lighting, moderate perspective, and one-sided lighting.
8. Record any `AMBIGUOUS`, `BLANK`, or `INVALID` result with its fill ratio,
   normalized darkness, contrast, combined score, evidence category, and
   quality fields before considering any threshold change.

P13.9.1 physical validation remains pending until this succeeds on the
printed page and device.
