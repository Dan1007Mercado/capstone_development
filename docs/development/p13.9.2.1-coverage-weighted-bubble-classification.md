# P13.9.2.1 Coverage-Weighted Bubble Classification

## Problem

The previous score weighted fill ratio at 0.40, normalized darkness at 0.35,
and darkness contrast at 0.25. The two correlated darkness inputs therefore
contributed 60% of the score and could let a small dark artifact compete with
a broader intentional fill.

The physical diagnostic example was Q4-B (`fillRatio 0.197`, contrast 39.25)
versus Q4-C (`fillRatio 0.439`, contrast 21.67). C had substantially greater
coverage but the old scores were close enough to produce ambiguity.

## Measurement feature

Bubble measurement now calculates `distributionScore` during the existing
inner-ROI pixel loop. The inner circle is divided into a configurable 3x3
grid. A cell is occupied when at least 12% of its valid pixels are foreground;
the score is occupied valid cells divided by all valid cells.

This adds two fixed arrays of nine integer counters per bubble. It performs no
connected-component analysis, second image pass, additional warp, or UI-isolate
image processing.

## Coverage-weighted scoring

The classifier exposes four explicit scores:

- `coverageScore = clamp(fillRatio / 0.45, 0, 1)`;
- `darknessScore = 0.50 * normalized-darkness component + 0.50 *
  local-contrast component`;
- `distributionScore`, measured by the fixed grid;
- `combinedMarkScore = 0.65 * coverageScore + 0.25 * darknessScore +
  0.10 * distributionScore`.

Coverage saturates at 0.45 because normal physical shading need not classify
every inner-ROI pixel as foreground. Darkness confirms ink but is no longer two
independent majority-weight inputs.

## Decision gates

The centralized `MarkClassificationConfig` retains raw darkness and quality
gates and adds:

- minimum/strong mark coverage: 0.12 / 0.45;
- conservative blank maximum coverage: 0.08;
- minimum darkness evidence: 0.22;
- minimum spread for a below-coverage mark: 0.45;
- minimum/strong combined score: 0.35 / 0.65;
- top/second score gap: 0.12;
- coverage dominance ratio/gap: 1.80 / 0.15;
- multiple-mark minimum coverage/confidence: 0.30 / 0.65.

Coverage dominance requires usable measurement quality, meaningful absolute
winner coverage, minimum darkness evidence, both relative and absolute
coverage separation, and a second candidate that does not itself pass the
genuine-multiple gates. It cannot suppress two independently strong fills.

Blank, conflicting, unreliable, and invalid evidence remain explicit.
Structured reasons now distinguish single strong marks, coverage dominance,
borderline marks, ambiguous candidates, unreliable baselines, multiple strong
marks, and unusable quality.

## Diagnostics

Per-bubble ROI diagnostics retain all raw measurements and additionally show
coverage, darkness, distribution, and combined mark scores. Question-level
diagnostics show the top and second candidates, score gap, coverage gap,
coverage ratio, final state, and structured reason. JSON and physical
validation records expose the same fields.

## Regression coverage

Tests protect conservative blanks, tiny dark dots, broadly distributed
moderate-darkness fills, the recorded Q4 B-vs-C evidence, two genuine strong
fills, close credible candidates, measurement distribution, multiple-choice
semantics, numeric grids, and scanner diagnostic serialization.

The constants are initial deterministic calibration values, not probability
thresholds. Printed-sheet retesting on the Infinix X6711 remains required,
especially for faint pencil, X/check marks, light partial fills, and varying
illumination. No physical fix is claimed until that testing is complete.
