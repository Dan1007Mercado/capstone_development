# P13.9.2 Canonical ROI and Measurement Diagnostics

## Current physical symptom

On the published `p11-1-scanner-validation` version 1 sheet, page 1 Q4-B
was fully filled with blue pen and then darkened with black ink but classified
`BLANK`. A small dark dot in neighboring Q4-C classified `MARKED`. Repeated
captures of unchanged marks have also alternated among `MARKED`, `AMBIGUOUS`,
and `BLANK`.

This phase adds evidence collection only. It does not claim or implement a
recognition fix.

## Diagnostic architecture

ROI diagnostics use the same selected published artifact, stored
`LayoutDocument`, camera still path, final scanner isolate, ArUco alignment,
homography, preprocessing, measurement, classification, and validation used by
normal scanning.

`OfflineScannerEngine` accepts an optional diagnostic sink. When requested, it
observes the successful production pipeline and returns a typed
`ScannerDiagnosticSnapshot` containing:

- the exact post-homography canonical pixels passed to preprocessing;
- the homography and alignment/reprojection diagnostics;
- the exact `BubbleMeasurementSet` passed into classification;
- the exact `PageClassificationResult` used to build `ScanResult`;
- the unchanged preprocessing, measurement, and classification configuration;
- scanner-stage timings and page identity.

Normal scans do not create or transfer this snapshot. Diagnostic scans make
one optional cross-isolate transfer of the canonical buffer. No second warp,
second preprocessing pass, or second measurement pass is performed.

## Canonical overlay

The ROI Diagnostics screen renders the canonical page with overlays sourced
directly from each production `BubbleMeasurement.roi`, which was calculated by
`CanonicalBubbleMapper` from the authoritative `PageLayout` and
`BubbleMeasurementConfig`:

- red point: calculated canonical center;
- translucent green disk: interior sampling ROI;
- yellow rings: inner and outer local-baseline radii;
- thin white outline: printed bubble radius;
- cyan outline/label: selected bubble.

The image is zoomable. Question and bubble selectors make Q4-A/B/C/D and page
2 Q6/Q7 directly inspectable.

## Evidence

Each bubble exposes canonical center and radii, fill ratio, normalized
darkness, darkness contrast, combined score, evidence category, decision
state, question state, interior mean and median, local baseline, foreground
count, baseline foreground ratio and reliability, valid-sample ratios, and
neighbor-mask count.

Structured JSON additionally includes template/version/page identity, marker
IDs, homography, reprojection errors, canonical dimensions, stage timings, and
scan sequence index. It also records the exact preprocessing, measurement, and
classification configuration used for that scan, including the existing
thresholds, plus sample counts and ROI clipping state.

## Repeated-scan workflow and memory

**Record Stability Scan** retains the latest five scans as numeric evidence
only. Per bubble it reports evidence for each scan, min/max values, category
changes, and question-state changes. The currently displayed snapshot alone
retains canonical pixels; historical scans retain no image buffers. Clearing
history removes all retained numeric records.

No automatic stability pass/fail threshold is introduced.

## Unchanged configuration

This phase changes no classifier thresholds or policies, ROI mapping math,
layout/PDF geometry, marker geometry, homography, preprocessing parameters,
camera readiness gates, or dense layout.

## Physical Q4-B versus Q4-C procedure

1. Open **Real Scanner Validation**.
2. Select `p11-1-scanner-validation` version 1.
3. Open **ROI Diagnostics**.
4. Align and capture page 1 with **Capture Diagnostic Scan**.
5. Select Q4, then compare Q4-B and Q4-C.
6. Verify the green interior disk is centered inside each printed bubble and
   the red center matches the printed center.
7. Check whether Q4-B's yellow baseline annulus intersects labels, marks, or
   other printed content differently from Q4-C.
8. Compare fill ratio, normalized darkness, darkness contrast, combined score,
   category, interior statistics, local baseline, foreground count, and
   quality fields.
9. Without changing the paper or marks, use **Record Stability Scan** five
   times.
10. Compare Q4-B and Q4-C min/max evidence, category changes, and question-state
    changes. Use **Show Diagnostic JSON** to preserve the structured values.

These observations can distinguish ROI placement or canonical drift from
preprocessing/baseline instability, measurement instability, or classification
after stable correct measurements. A root cause must not be assigned until the
printed sheet is tested on the Infinix X6711.
