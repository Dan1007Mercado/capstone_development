# OMR Validation Matrix

The production scanner should eventually be evaluated against combinations of the following.

## Mark Types

- strong pencil fill
- light pencil fill
- black pen
- blue pen
- partial fill
- check mark
- cross mark
- erased response
- double mark
- blank response

## Camera Conditions

- direct top-down
- moderate perspective
- larger perspective
- rotated page
- page near camera
- page farther away
- mild blur
- motion blur

## Lighting

- bright uniform light
- dim light
- one-sided shadow
- brightness gradient
- hotspot
- warm light
- cool light

## Paper

- flat
- slight fold
- mild curl
- slightly dirty
- photocopy where supported

## Printing

- different printer models
- grayscale printing
- different quality settings
- slight scale variation

## Device Testing

Test more than one Android device before claiming broad compatibility.
